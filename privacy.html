Privacy Policy for Tadaao

Last Updated: September 26, 2026
Effective Date: September 26, 2026

Welcome to Tadaao. Privacy is a foundational principle of this application. This Privacy Policy explains how Tadaao handles, uses, and safeguards information across mobile and desktop devices.

1. Core Philosophy: Peer-to-Peer & Privacy by Design

Tadaao is designed from the ground up as a direct, decentralized, local peer-to-peer (P2P) file sharing and media streaming utility.

  • No Cloud Uploads: Files, media, and room messages are transferred directly between devices over your local Wi-Fi network, Wi-Fi Direct, or Local Area Network (LAN).
  • No Central Tracking Servers: Tadaao does not host files, intercept streams, or store copies of data on external servers.
  • Your Data Remains Yours: All communications occur locally between the sender and receiver.

2. Information Accessed and How It Is Used

To perform file transfers, media streaming, and local room communication, Tadaao requires access to certain device features and system permissions. These are used strictly for local functionality:

Category Technical Detail Purpose & Usage
Local Network & Wi-Fi ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE ACCESS_WIFI_STATE CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES Used to discover nearby devices running Tadaao on the same local network and establish direct high-speed P2P connections.
Files & Storage READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE READ_MEDIA_* Required only when you select files, photos, audio, or videos to send, and to save received files into your chosen download directory.
Camera android.permission.CAMERA Used solely to scan QR codes for quick pairing, room joining, or device connection verification. No photos or videos from the camera are captured or stored without your explicit action.
Microphone / Audio android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Used only if you choose to record and send voice messages or audio clips within local room chats. Audio is only processed when you record and is sent directly to connected peers.
Local Storage / Database SQLite (sqflite) Stores your local preferences (such as auto-accept settings, trusted devices, download directory) and transfer history locally on your device. This data never leaves your device.

3. Data Retention and Storage

  • Files & Media: All transferred files, photos, videos, and documents reside exclusively on the sender's and receiver's devices.
  • Chat Messages: Messages sent in local chat/rooms are transient or stored locally on the participating devices.
  • No Cloud Backups: Tadaao operates zero cloud servers for backing up or retaining transfers. Deleting a file or uninstalling the app removes local history and preferences according to device operating system behavior.

4. Security and Device Control

Tadaao provides built-in mechanisms to give you control over incoming connections and transfers:

  • Explicit Transfer Approvals: By default, you must approve incoming file transfer requests before receiving data.
  • Trusted Devices: You can mark specific devices as "Trusted" or revoke trust at any time in the app settings.
  • Room PINs & Pairing: Direct room connections and sessions support optional PIN protection to prevent unauthorized joins on the local network.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

  • No Advertising Trackers: Tadaao does not integrate third-party ad networks or tracking SDKs that profile behavior.
  • No Personal Data Brokerage: Personal data or file contents are never sold, rented, monetized, or disclosed to third parties.

6. Children’s Privacy

Tadaao does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because the app does not collect personal identities or maintain user accounts, children's data is neither collected nor stored on remote servers.

7. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can clear your transfer history directly within the History screen.
  • You can manage or revoke trusted devices in the Settings screen.
  • You can revoke app permissions (Storage, Camera, Microphone) at any time via your device's system settings.
  • Uninstalling the application deletes all locally stored app databases and preferences.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
